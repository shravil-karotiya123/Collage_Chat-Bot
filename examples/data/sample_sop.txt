====================================================
MRPL REFINERY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
====================================================
SOP Code: MRPL-SOP-502
Title: Non-Destructive Inspection & Pressure Equipment Approval Governance

1. PURPOSE
Defines mandatory procedures for inspecting, evaluating, and documenting pressure equipment across MRPL refineries.

2. GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENT
- All technical inspection findings must include reference identifier MRPL-AI-DEMO-001.
- Official Approval Notes require review by the Chief Technical Inspector before execution.
- Workflows must maintain 100% air-gapped sovereign execution.
